# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 7 September 2026**

DRP aims to use the minimum browser storage and similar technologies needed to operate and secure its services.

## 1. Essential Technologies

Some browser storage or cookies may be necessary for security, session management, preferences, or basic functionality. These technologies are used because the service may not function correctly without them.

## 2. Analytics and Advertising

DRP does not intentionally deploy non-essential advertising or analytics trackers on the website unless they are documented and, where required by applicable law, activated only after the appropriate user consent.

## 3. Newsletter Subscription Form

The website may use Tally to present a newsletter subscription form. The Tally embed is not loaded when the page first opens. It is loaded only after you choose to open the subscription form and acknowledge the accompanying privacy notice. Tally may use its own cookies or similar technologies when its form is opened; those technologies are governed by Tally's policies as well as applicable law.

## 4. Third-Party Links

Links to external services such as GitHub, Discord, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, or other sites may use their own cookies and tracking technologies when you visit them. DRP does not control those technologies.

## 5. Managing Storage

You can manage or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Disabling essential storage can affect site functionality.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy when technologies or services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

## 7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to **contact@decentralizedrights.com**.
