# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 7 September 2026**

Decentralized Rights Protocol (DRP) is committed to collecting and using only the information reasonably needed to operate its websites, applications, community services, and protocol-related features.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the feature you use, DRP may process information you choose to provide, such as an email address when you subscribe to updates, wallet addresses or public blockchain identifiers when you use protocol features, and information included in support or partnership messages.

For proof or verification features, additional evidence may be submitted by you. Do not submit private keys, seed phrases, passwords, financial credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Our websites may also receive ordinary technical information required to deliver and secure the service, such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs, and security events.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to provide requested services, operate and secure DRP, respond to inquiries, process subscriptions, improve documentation and user experience, investigate abuse, comply with applicable law, and maintain protocol records.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Some features may use third-party providers. For example, the DRP website may use Tally to present a newsletter subscription form. The Tally form is loaded only after you explicitly choose to open the subscription flow. Third-party providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Blockchain and Decentralized Records

Blockchain networks and decentralized storage can create records that are difficult or impossible to alter or delete. Do not publish personal information that you do not want associated with a public or decentralized record. Where appropriate, DRP aims to use hashes, commitments, or references instead of placing unnecessary personal information directly on public ledgers.

## 5. Data Minimization and Retention

DRP aims to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal obligations, security, dispute handling, and legitimate operational needs. Retention can vary by service and by whether information forms part of a decentralized or public record.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and service involved. No internet-connected system or decentralized network can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and technical limitations, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by DRP. Requests involving immutable or decentralized records may require an explanation of what can and cannot technically be changed.

## 8. Ghana Data Protection

Where applicable, DRP seeks to operate consistently with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), including principles of lawful processing, purpose limitation, data minimization, transparency, security, and accountability.

## 9. International Processing

DRP and its service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be considered for such transfers.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **contact@decentralizedrights.com**.

This policy describes current practices and may be updated when the services or applicable legal requirements change.
